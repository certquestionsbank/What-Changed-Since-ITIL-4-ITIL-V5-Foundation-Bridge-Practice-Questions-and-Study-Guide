# What-Changed-Since-ITIL-4-ITIL-V5-Foundation-Bridge-Practice-Questions-and-Study-Guide
ITIL Foundation Bridge (Version 5) is PeopleCert's focused route for eligible ITIL 4 certification holders to earn ITIL Foundation (Version 5). It concentrates on the updates rather than asking candidates to repeat the entire Foundation course. This guide uses the official PeopleCert product page and the verified shared knowledge-base outline.

## Exam Introduction and Overview

PeopleCert lists **20 multiple-choice questions**, **30 minutes**, a **closed-book** format, and a **65% minimum passing score**. The exam is offered in English, Chinese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), and Spanish.

Eligibility matters. PeopleCert requires an ITIL 4 certification, except ITIL 4 Specialist: Acquiring and Managing Cloud Services and ITIL 4 Specialist: Sustainability in Digital and IT. It says results will not be released without proof of the qualifying certification.

“ITIL V5 Foundation Bridge” is a convenient local subject label. The official title is **ITIL Foundation Bridge (Version 5)**. The cited PeopleCert page does not display a separate exam code.

## Official Learning Areas

PeopleCert describes five areas of focus. These are learning areas from the public page, **not** scored domains with published weights:

1. **Key enhancements in ITIL (Version 5):** new and refined concepts, terminology, and structural updates since ITIL 4.
2. **Digital product and service management evolution:** how the guidance addresses modern digital and AI-enabled environments across the lifecycle.
3. **Updated language and core concepts:** the terminology and principles teams use to discuss the new version.
4. **Enhanced value creation approach:** connections among stakeholders, strategy, delivery, and measurable outcomes.
5. **Applying ITIL (Version 5) in practice:** using the changes to improve collaboration and results.

The public page does not publish detailed numbered exam objectives or domain percentages. Study these areas as a scope map, not as a weighting table.

## Preparation Tips

Start with what you already know from ITIL 4. Build a two-column notebook: an existing concept on the left and the Version 5 update on the right. Add a short example showing why the update matters in a digital product or service environment. This prevents the Bridge syllabus from dissolving into a full ITIL 4 revision.

Next, write a scenario involving a digital service, several stakeholders, and a measurable outcome. Explain how updated terminology, lifecycle thinking, and value creation would change the conversation. When you cannot explain a distinction in plain language, return to PeopleCert's official learning material.

## How to Use ITIL V5 Foundation Bridge Practice Questions

CertQuestionsBank [ITIL V5 Foundation Bridge practice questions](https://www.certquestionsbank.com/ITIL-V5-Foundation-Bridge.html) can help structure a short review session. Read the explanation after every answer, label the update it tests, and check the official PeopleCert material when a term is unfamiliar. Independent practice questions are study aids, not PeopleCert exam questions, and they cannot guarantee an outcome.

## Importance of the Certification

The Bridge route lets eligible ITIL 4 holders demonstrate familiarity with the Version 5 changes. It is relevant to service managers, product owners, operations specialists, and others who need a shared vocabulary for digital products and services.
